FAQ

Common Questions

Is IGotDomain really free?
Yes — free forever. Every feature and AI conversation is free, and we never require a credit card. We may, in future, earn a small affiliate referral from providers you choose, but this never changes which options we surface or their ranking.
Do you sell my data or my email?
Never. Your email is used only to save your launch progress so you can return later. We don't sell leads, and we don't share your details with third parties for marketing.
Are your tool comparisons sponsored?
No. Our domain, website builder, business account and accounting comparisons are unbiased and data-driven. We rank by what's genuinely best for your situation — not by who pays us.
How does the launch process work?
You enter your email to save progress, share your idea or a domain, then we suggest names and compare prices and tools. Your Business Launch Score tracks you from 0% to 100% — see How It Works.
Is the comparison data real?
The current demo uses realistic sample data so you can see the full experience. In production these figures are pulled live from each provider.
