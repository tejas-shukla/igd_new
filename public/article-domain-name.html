How to Choose the Perfect Domain Name for Your Startup

P
Priya Sharma
Updated April 2026 · 8 min read

Your domain is the single most repeated piece of your brand. People type it, say it out loud, and judge your credibility by it. Yet most founders pick one in a panic the night before launch. Here's how to choose well — and how to avoid overpaying.

What Makes a Great Domain

  • Short and memorable. Aim for under 15 characters. If you have to spell it out on a call, it's too clever.
  • Easy to say and type. Avoid hyphens, numbers and words people misspell.
  • Brandable over descriptive. "Stripe" beats "online-payments-processing" every time.
  • Future-proof. Don't box yourself in with a name that won't fit your next product.

The .com vs .io vs .ai Debate

A .com still carries the most trust and is the default people type. But great .coms are scarce and expensive. A .io has become completely normal for tech and SaaS, and .ai signals exactly what you'd expect. The right answer depends on your audience:

If your customers are mainstream consumers, fight for the .com. If they're technical or startup-native, a clean .io or .ai is perfectly credible.

SEO: Does the Extension Matter?

Not much. Search engines rank content and authority, not your TLD. A memorable .io with great content will out-rank a clunky keyword-stuffed .com. Don't sacrifice brandability chasing an exact-match keyword domain — that era is long over.

The Hidden Cost: Renewals

This is where founders lose money. Registrars love a cheap first-year price, then quietly hike the renewal. A domain advertised at a few dollars can renew at three or four times that. Two providers — Cloudflare and Porkbun — are known for selling at or near cost with no renewal markup, which adds up over the life of your business.

Always compare before you buy

  • Check the first-year price and the renewal price side by side.
  • Confirm WHOIS privacy is included free (it should be).
  • Watch for upsell-heavy checkouts that pre-tick paid add-ons.
